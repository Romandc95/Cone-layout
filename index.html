Cone Layout

Concentric cone and reducer flat patterns from three numbers. Lay it out with a tape and square off a straight baseline. No 50-inch trammel, no guess and check.

Enter both diameters and the length along the centerline. Thickness matters on the layout: the pattern is laid out on the mean diameter so it closes after rolling. Accepts 12, 12.5, 12 1/2 or 12-1/2.

Layout method

    Every station is measured both ways from the centerline. "Up" goes to the large-end arc, "down" goes to the small-end arc. Past the small-end corner the bottom edge is the straight side, shown in grey for checking.

      Layout is figured on the mean (centerline) diameter: outside Ø minus one thickness, or inside Ø plus one thickness. That's what makes thin-gauge stainless close up without a gap or overlap after rolling. Seam lap is added to one straight edge of each piece.
